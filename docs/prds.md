## 1. Auto-apply for testers beyond Eric

**Who:** beta testers who already have a profile and resume.
**Today:** they read postings, rewrite the resume and cover letter for each one, and fill the same forms again. The effort per job is the same every time, and most applications get nothing back.
**It worked if:** testers press Submit on 70% or more of the drafts Lookout prepares, after edits, within two weeks. That number is the trust signal.
**Smallest version:** drafts only, for 4s and 5s. The tester sees exactly what will be entered, edits it, asks for a revision, goes back to an earlier version, and presses Submit. The browser extension fills the form, so it looks like the person, not a bot. Spec: [auto-apply.md](auto-apply.md).
**Never:** submit without the person pressing Submit. Invent a win or a number. Sound like AI slop.
**Guess, legal call (#29):** open it to testers once #29's list is done: the person approves each application, Lookout keeps a record of exactly what was submitted, the Terms say the user is the applicant and responsible for accuracy, and any site whose terms forbid automated submission gets the Manual submit checklist instead of the extension. The lawyer review (#22) waits until strangers can sign up.
**Guess, who gets it first:** one tester who already has a full profile and resume, for two weeks, before anyone else.
