# Code of Conduct

Lookout follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing someone's private information aren't accepted, in issues, pull requests or anywhere the project is discussed.

## Reporting

Email ericdevlin@me.com. Reports are read by the maintainer only and handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments or contributions that break this code, and may ban someone from the project for repeated or serious violations.
